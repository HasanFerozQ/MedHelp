The Process

How to Audit Your Medical Bill

1

Request Your Itemized Bill

Call the hospital billing department and ask specifically for an "itemized statement." This lists every charge individually — which is what you need to audit.

2

Enter Line Items Into Our Audit Tool

Use MediHelp's free Bill Audit tool to enter each charge. Our engine automatically cross-checks CPT codes, Medicare benchmarks, and bundling rules.

3

Review Red-Flagged Findings

Errors are highlighted in real-time with an explanation of the issue and the estimated overcharge amount. You can export a dispute letter directly from the tool.

4

Dispute or Get Expert Help

Use the dispute letter template to challenge errors yourself, or open a case with our human negotiation service for complex, high-balance bills.
