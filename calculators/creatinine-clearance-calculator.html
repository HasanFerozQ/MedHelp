Creatinine Clearance Calculator (Cockcroft-Gault)

Estimated creatinine clearance to guide dosing (Cockcroft-Gault).

Formula

CrCl (mL/min) = ((140 − age) × weight (kg)) / (72 × SCr (mg/dL)) × (0.85 if female)

Interpretation

Used to adjust drug dosing and assess renal function; correlate with eGFR where appropriate.

Clinical notes

Use ideal or adjusted body weight for obese patients; Cockcroft-Gault estimates differ from CKD-EPI.

Examples

Example: 65 y/o male, 70 kg, SCr 1.2 mg/dL → CrCl ≈ ((140-65)*70)/(72*1.2)= 56.9 mL/min

FAQs

When should I use adjusted body weight? For BMI >30 consider adjusted weight for dosing certain drugs.
