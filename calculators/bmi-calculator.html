BMI Calculator — Body Mass Index (BMI)

Calculate BMI using height and weight to estimate body fat for adults. Use this tool as a screening measure — interpret clinically.

Definition

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple index of weight-for-height commonly used to classify underweight, overweight and obesity in adults.

Formula

BMI = weight (kg) / (height (m))²

Interpretation & Normal ranges

  • Underweight: < 18.5
  • Normal weight: 18.5–24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0–29.9
  • Obesity Class I: 30.0–34.9
  • Obesity Class II: 35.0–39.9
  • Obesity Class III: ≥ 40.0

Clinical use

Useful for population screening and risk stratification. BMI has limitations in athletes, pregnancy, and varies with age and ethnicity.

Examples

Example: weight 70 kg, height 1.75 m → BMI = 70 / (1.75²) = 22.9 kg/m² (Normal)

FAQs

What is a normal BMI?

18.5–24.9 is considered normal for most adults.

How accurate is BMI?

BMI is a proxy for body fat and does not distinguish muscle from fat.

Can BMI be used in athletes?

In athletes with high muscle mass BMI may overestimate adiposity.
