# FDA Clears First AI Test to Guide Breast Cancer Chemo Decisions — What It Means for Patients

For the millions of women diagnosed with breast cancer each year, one of the most agonizing decisions comes early: do I need chemotherapy?

Chemotherapy is powerful — but it comes with a heavy cost. Hair loss, fatigue, nausea, weakened immunity, and in some cases, long-term heart or nerve damage. For decades, doctors have used genomic tests to help decide who needs chemo and who can safely skip it. But those tests can be expensive, slow, and sometimes leave patients in a "gray zone" of uncertainty.

That changed this week.

The FDA has officially cleared **ArteraAI Breast**, the first-ever AI-powered prognostic tool designed to help clinicians and patients navigate the chemotherapy decision for early-stage, localized breast cancer.

<div class="key-takeaway">
  <strong>🔍 Core Finding</strong>
  ArteraAI Breast uses artificial intelligence to analyze standard pathology slides (H&E) to predict a patient's benefit from chemotherapy, providing a faster and potentially more accessible alternative to traditional genomic testing.
</div>
