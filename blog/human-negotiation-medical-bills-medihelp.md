# Human Negotiation for Medical Bills: What MediHelp Does and Why It Works

Medical bills are confusing by design. Most patients see codes, totals, and due dates but cannot see the pricing logic behind those numbers. That is where **MediHelp Human Negotiation** comes in.

We do not just send a template email. We combine **structured audit intelligence** with **real human advocacy** to push for lower balances and cleaner billing outcomes.

![Medical billing paperwork and review](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1576671081837-49000212a370?w=1200&q=80&auto=format&fit=crop)

## What we do for you

### 1) Audit first, negotiate second
Every negotiation starts with a technical review of your bill lines:

- code-level mismatch checks
- duplicate and bundling risk detection
- pricing variance vs benchmark logic
- support evidence from your bill + EOB documents

This gives us a concrete case strategy before outreach begins.

### 2) Build the negotiation case
We organize your file into a dispute-ready structure:

- key overcharge signals
- disputed service lines
- documentation and correction requests
- clear argument trail for billing office follow-up

### 3) Communicate with billing teams
MediHelp then supports provider-facing dispute communication and follow-through, so you are not doing this alone.

You should consider escalation when:
