Front: Definition of Hypertrophy?

Back: Increase in the size of cells resulting in an increase in the size of the organ; no new cells.

Front: Definition of Hyperplasia?

Back: Increase in the number of cells in an organ or tissue in response to a stimulus.

Front: What is Metaplasia?

Back: A reversible change in which one adult cell type is replaced by another adult cell type (e.g., Barrett esophagus).

Front: Hallmarks of irreversible cell injury?

Back: Profound mitochondrial dysfunction and severe disturbances in membrane function (plasma and lysosomal).
