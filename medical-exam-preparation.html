S1

USMLE Step 1

The United States Medical Licensing Examination Step 1 evaluates understanding of foundational medical sciences. It is one of the most high-stakes examinations in medicine, tested in a single 8-hour session of 280 questions organised into blocks. A strong Step 1 score requires deep integration across basic science disciplines — you must be able to trace a biochemical defect to its clinical manifestation and back again.

MediHelp's USMLE Step 1 question bank is organised by discipline and system, with detailed answer explanations that teach the underlying mechanism rather than just the correct answer choice. Every question is tagged by organ system and competency level.

S2

USMLE Step 2 CK

Step 2 Clinical Knowledge shifts from basic sciences to clinical medicine. You are expected to demonstrate the ability to diagnose, manage, and prevent conditions seen by physicians in outpatient, inpatient, and emergency settings. The emphasis is on selecting the most appropriate next step — not the most interesting — from a clinical management standpoint.

Our Step 2 CK preparation includes 900+ clinical vignettes mapped to the core clinical disciplines tested on the actual examination, with explanations that follow the USMLE's preferred management algorithms.

S3

USMLE Step 3

Step 3 is the final examination in the USMLE series and tests independent patient management. Unlike Steps 1 and 2, Step 3 includes Computer-based Case Simulations (CCS) in addition to multiple-choice questions. You must demonstrate the ability to initiate workups, order treatments, and manage patients over time across ambulatory and inpatient environments.

MediHelp's Step 3 preparation resources include Foundations of Independent Practice (FIP) questions and Advanced Clinical Medicine (ACM) vignettes, covering the full breadth of physician management scenarios.

MB

MBBS Professional Examinations

Medical school professional examinations vary by country and institution, but consistently test preclinical and clinical sciences across multiple professional years. First and second year examinations focus heavily on anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, and pathology. Third and fourth year examinations shift toward clinical subjects including medicine, surgery, pediatrics, and obstetrics.

MediHelp's MBBS year-wise question bank allows students to study by subject within each professional year. Our content is curated from internationally recognised textbooks and aligned with common examination formats used in Pakistan, India, UK, Bangladesh, Egypt, Nigeria, and Malaysia.

PL

PLAB (Professional and Linguistic Assessments Board)

The PLAB examination is the route by which international medical graduates demonstrate that they have the knowledge, skills, and experience to practise medicine safely in the UK. PLAB 1 is a written examination of 180 single best answer (SBA) questions covering clinical medicine in a UK context. PLAB 2 is an objective structured clinical examination (OSCE).

PLAB 1 success requires familiarity with UK clinical guidelines (NICE guidelines), British drug formulary (BNF), and NHS clinical pathways. Questions follow clinical scenarios that reflect the day-to-day decisions of a foundation year doctor in a UK hospital setting.

MediHelp provides PLAB-style SBA questions with explanations grounded in NICE and UK clinical practice, helping candidates develop the clinical decision-making framework that PLAB 1 rewards.

AM

AMC (Australian Medical Council) Examination

The AMC examination is the pathway for international medical graduates seeking registration to practise medicine in Australia. The AMC Computer Adaptive Test (CAT) is a multiple-choice examination testing the application of medical knowledge in the Australian healthcare context. The subsequent AMC Clinical Examination assesses clinical skills through structured scenarios.

AMC MCQ preparation requires familiarity with Australian clinical practice guidelines, MBS (Medicare Benefits Schedule), and the priorities of the Australian healthcare system, including preventive care, indigenous health, and rural medicine. MediHelp's clinical question bank covers the primary clinical domains tested in the AMC CAT.

General Practice Internal Medicine Surgery Paediatrics Psychiatry Obstetrics & Gynaecology Emergency Medicine Preventive & Rural Medicine

MR

MRCP (Membership of the Royal Colleges of Physicians)

The MRCP(UK) examination is an internationally respected postgraduate medical qualification required for specialist training in internal medicine in the United Kingdom and many Commonwealth countries. The examination comprises three parts: MRCP Part 1, MRCP Part 2 Written (Best of Five questions), and MRCP PACES (Practical Assessment of Clinical Examination Skills).

MRCP Part 1 tests the scientific basis of clinical medicine across major medical specialities. MRCP Part 2 Written demands mastery of complex clinical scenarios involving multimorbid patients with diagnostic uncertainty. Both parts use Best of Five (BoF) format, testing clinical reasoning rather than pure recall.

MediHelp's clinical question bank includes BoF-style questions mapped to the MRCP syllabus across Cardiology, Gastroenterology, Respiratory Medicine, Neurology, Rheumatology, Nephrology, Endocrinology, Haematology, and Infectious Diseases.
