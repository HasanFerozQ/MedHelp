Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MediHelp Now ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle data when you use our website (medihelp.live) and Chrome Extension.

2. Zero-Server Local Audit Architecture

MediHelp is built on a "Privacy First" architecture. Unlike other platforms, our Elite Audit Engine executes entirely within your web browser. This means your medical bills are never uploaded to our servers, and we never store your health data.

3. Transparency & Security

Registry lookups (like NPI verification) are performed via public APIs, but your sensitive itemized bill data stays strictly on your device.

4. v5.0 Managed Advocacy & Data Hygiene (HIPAA Hygiene)

For users who choose our Human Negotiation service, we implement rigorous data hygiene protocols to protect your clinical information:

  • 12-Hour Zero-Retention: All itemized bills, medical records, and government identification scans uploaded for intake are permanently scrubbed from our secure intake vault within 12 hours of the intake email being finalized.
  • End-to-End Encryption: Intake bundles are password-protected using your unique Case ID, ensuring that only authorized clinical advocates can access the documentation.
  • Identity Data: Government IDs are used solely for the "Legal Power of Attorney" verification required by hospitals and are purged immediately after verification.

3. What Information We Do Collect

  • Website Analytics via Google Analytics: We use standard analytics to understand how visitors interact with medihelp.live (e.g., page views, browser type). This data is anonymized.
  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Extension Usage: We do not track individual usage of the extension.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

MediHelp Now uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed. This information helps us optimize the user experience. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. USMLE, MBBS & Medical Exam Prep Data

To provide a personalized learning experience, MediHelp stores your study progress (e.g., correct/incorrect question counts) strictly within your own browser's LocalStorage. This data resides on your device and is not transmitted to, collected by, or stored on MediHelp’s servers. Clearing your browser cache or site data will remove this progress.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our repository issue tracker or official contact methods.
