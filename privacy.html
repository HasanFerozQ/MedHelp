Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MediHelp Now ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle data when you use our website (medihelp.live) and Chrome Extension.

2. Absolutely No Patient Data Sent to Servers

Our core philosophy is HIPAA-compliance by design. When you use the MediHelp Now Chrome Extension to audit medical bills or search codes, all processing happens locally on your computer. We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit any Protected Health Information (PHI) or Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to our servers.

3. What Information We Do Collect

  • Website Analytics via Google Analytics: We use standard analytics to understand how visitors interact with medihelp.live (e.g., page views, browser type). This data is anonymized.
  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Extension Usage: We do not track individual usage of the extension.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

MediHelp Now uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed. This information helps us optimize the user experience. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our repository issue tracker or official contact methods.
