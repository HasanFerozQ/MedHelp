What is an NPI Number?

An NPI (National Provider Identifier) is a unique identification number assigned to healthcare providers in the United States. It was mandated by the HIPAA Administrative Simplification Standard.

The NPI number is used for:

  • Accurate medical billing across systems
  • Insurance claim submission and processing
  • Standardized provider identification and verification

Why Use an NPI Lookup Tool?

Searching NPI numbers is a critical step in verifying the medical care you receive and the accuracy of your financial statements. Using our tool helps you:

  • Verify healthcare providers: Confirm providers are registered with CMS.
  • Avoid billing errors: Ensure the NPI on your bill matches the provider you saw.
  • Confirm provider identity: Protect yourself against healthcare identity fraud.
  • Accurate insurance claims: Prevent claim denials due to provider mismatches.

How to Find a Provider Using NPI

To find a healthcare provider using their NPI number or name, follow these 3 steps:

  • Enter credentials: Input the provider's first/last name or their 10-digit NPI number.
  • Review records: Examine the practice address, NPI status, and taxonomy codes.
  • Confirm specialty: Verify the provider's medical specialty matches the service rendered.

Audit Tip: Specialty mismatches (e.g., a General Practitioner billing as a Specialist) are a common cause of overcharging.
