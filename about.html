About Us

A Patient-First Platform for Healthcare Clarity

MediHelp is a patient-first platform dedicated to demystifying medical billing and providing accessible clinical tools. We help individuals audit hospital bills, verify charges, appeal denials, and reduce out-of-pocket costs using reliable, CMS-backed resources.

We believe every person — regardless of their insurance literacy or income — deserves access to the same tools professionals use. That's why we've built everything from our bill audit engine to our 150+ clinical calculators to be free, fast, and private by design.

Core Principles

Our Values & Approach

Every decision at MediHelp is guided by four principles that keep us independent and patient-focused.

🎯

Accuracy & Evidence-Based

All tools and content draw from official sources including CMS Medicare fee schedules, NCCI edits, and peer-reviewed medical guidelines. We prioritize primary sources over secondary interpretations.

🔒

Privacy First

Local browser-based processing for bill audits — your sensitive itemized data never leaves your device. Registry lookups use public APIs only. We do not store personal health information.

💡

Transparency

Clear pricing, no hidden fees, and prominent disclaimers on all tools and calculators. Any promotional content is clearly labeled and will never influence our editorial decisions.

👥

User-Focused

Designed for patients, students, nurses, physicians, and anyone navigating medical costs. No jargon, no paywalls, no signup required for our free tools — just answers when you need them.

Our Team

Team & Expertise

Our content and tools are developed and reviewed by professionals with backgrounds in healthcare administration, medical billing, and clinical practice. We collaborate with subject-matter experts to ensure information is current, reliable, and actionable.
