## CASE 01 — Two Months of Low Mood and Anhedonia
Scenario: A 28-year-old woman has 2 months of depressed mood, anhedonia, low energy, guilt, poor concentration, and early-morning awakening. No mania/hypomania history.

Q1. Most likely diagnosis?  
A. Adjustment disorder with depressed mood

B. CAGE
