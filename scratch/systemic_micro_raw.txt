Front: Primary infection site for Blastomyces dermatitidis?

Back: Lungs (often disseminates to skin and bone).

Clinical Microbiology: The "Most Commons"

Front: Most common cause of meningitis in adults (all ages combined)?

Back: Streptococcus pneumoniae.

Front: Most common cause of meningitis in teenagers/young adults in dorms?

Back: Neisseria meningitidis.

Front: Most common cause of UTIs in young, sexually active females (second to E. coli)?

Back: Staphylococcus saprophyticus.

Front: Most common cause of fungal endocarditis in IV drug users?

Back: Candida albicans.

Bacterial & Viral Properties (Deep Cut)

Front: Which bacteria contains a "Vi capsular antigen"?

Back: Salmonella Typhi.

Front: Mechanism of the "Toxic Shock Syndrome Toxin-1" (TSST-1)?

Back: Superantigen that binds to MHC II and T-cell receptor (TCR) simultaneously, causing massive IFN-&gamma; and IL-2 release.

Front: Identification of Enterococcus species on agar?

Back: Growth in 6.5% NaCl and ability to hydrolyze esculin in the presence of bile (Bile Esculin agar).

Front: Only RNA virus with a "bullet-shaped" envelope?

Back: Rabies virus (Rhabdovirus).

Front: Virus that uses the "CCR5" or "CXCR4" co-receptors for entry?

Back: HIV (gp120 binds to these after binding CD4).

Parasitology & Helminths

Front: "Loeffler syndrome" is associated with the pulmonary phase of which parasite?

Back: Ascaris lumbricoides.

Front: Parasite associated with "Iron Deficiency Anemia" due to blood sucking in the GI tract?

Back: Ancylostoma duodenale or Necator americanus (Hookworms).

Front: Diagnosis of Schistosoma mansoni via stool sample?

Back: Eggs with a prominent "Lateral Spine."

Front: Diagnosis of Schistosoma haematobium via urine sample?

Back: Eggs with a prominent "Terminal Spine."

Front: Treatment for most Trematodes (Flukes) and Cestodes (Tapeworms)?

Back: Praziquantel.

Laboratory Tests & Stains (The Boards Favorite)

Front: "Elek test" is used to detect the toxin of which organism?

Back: Corynebacterium diphtheriae.

Front: "Warthin-Starry stain" (silver stain) is used to visualize which two common pathogens?

Back: Bartonella henselae and Helicobacter pylori.

Front: The "Nagler Test" for C. perfringens detects the activity of?

Back: Alpha-toxin (Lecithinase).

Front: Media used to isolate Vibrio cholerae from stool?

Back: TCBS (Thiosulfate-Citrate-Bile salts-Sucrose) agar.

Front: "Chocolate agar" contains which two specific factors?

Back: Factor X (Hematin) and Factor V (NAD).

Antibiotics: Resistance & Specifics

Front: Resistance to Methicillin (MRSA) is mediated by alterations in?

Back: Penicillin-Binding Proteins (PBP 2a), encoded by the mecA gene.

Front: Mechanism of Vancomycin resistance in Enterococci (VRE)?

Back: Alteration of the D-Ala-D-Ala terminal to D-Ala-D-Lac.

Front: Drug of choice for Lyme Disease in a child < 8 years old?

Back: Amoxicillin (Doxycycline is avoided due to tooth discoloration).

Front: Antibiotic that can cause "Gray Baby Syndrome"?

Back: Chloramphenicol (due to lack of liver UDP-glucuronosyltransferase).

Front: Antibiotic class that can cause "Tendon Rupture" (Achilles)?

Back: Fluoroquinolones.

Clinical Micro "Rules of Thumb"

Front: Infection following a "Human Bite"?

Back: Eikenella corrodens.

Front: Infection following a "Clutch of Chickens" or "Reptiles"?

Back: Non-typhoidal Salmonella.

Front: Infection following "Skinning a Rabbit"?

Back: Francisella tularensis.

Front: Infection following "Exposure to Parrot/Bird Feces"?

Back: Chlamydia psittaci.

Final High-Yield Virology

Front: Virus that causes "Aplastic Crisis" in patients with hereditary spherocytosis?

Back: Parvovirus B19.

Front: Primary cause of "Hemorrhagic Fever" with renal syndrome?

Back: Hantavirus (Bunyavirus).

Front: The only "Live Attenuated" vaccine given to HIV patients (if CD4 > 200)?

Back: MMR and Varicella.

Front: "Koplik spots" are a prodromal sign of?

Back: Measles (Rubeola).
