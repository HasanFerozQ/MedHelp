Back: Indicates underlying Ductal Carcinoma In Situ (DCIS).

Advanced Concepts & Syndromes

Front: Common genetic mutation in Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome (PCOS)?

Back: Hyperinsulinemia leading to deranged LH:FSH ratio (>2:1).

Front: Classic clinical pentad of PCOS?

Back: Obesity, hirsutism, acne, infertility, and oligomenorrhea.

Front: Association of Lichen Sclerosus with other diseases?

Back: Autoimmune disorders (e.g., thyroiditis, vitiligo).

Front: Role of High-risk HPV (16, 18) in oncogenesis?

Back: E6 protein inhibits p53; E7 protein inhibits Rb.

Front: Difference between a follicular cyst and a corpus luteum cyst?

Back: Follicular arises from unruptured follicle; Corpus Luteum arises from a failed involution of the corpus luteum.

Front: Histologic appearance of Medullary Carcinoma of the breast?

Back: Sheets of large cells with prominent lymphocytic infiltrate (associated with BRCA1).

Front: Most common cause of Scrotal Swelling in infants?

Back: Hydrocele (patent processus vaginalis).

Front: Mechanism of a Varicocele?

Back: Dilated veins in the pampiniform plexus ("bag of worms").

Front: Why are Varicoceles more common on the left side?

Back: The left spermatic vein drains into the left renal vein at a 90-degree angle.

Front: Definition of Hypospadias?

Back: Urethral opening on the ventral (bottom) surface of the penis.

Front: Definition of Epispadias?

Back: Urethral opening on the dorsal (top) surface of the penis (associated with bladder exstrophy).

Front: Definition of Bowen Disease of the penis?

Back: In situ Squamous Cell Carcinoma presenting as a leukoplakic plaque on the shaft.

Front: Major risk factor for Penile Squamous Cell Carcinoma?

Back: HPV infection and lack of circumcision (poor hygiene/smegma).

Front: Hallmark of Sertoli-Leydig Cell Tumors?

Back: Re-masculinization or virilization in females.

Front: Pathology of Brenner Tumor?

Back: Ovarian tumor containing "Walnut-like" nuclei and urothelial (bladder-like) epithelium.

Front: Definition of Endometrial Polyp?

Back: Benign exophytic growth of endometrial glands and stroma; often associated with Tamoxifen.

Front: Mechanism of fat necrosis in the breast?

Back: Trauma-induced release of fatty acids leading to saponification and calcification.

Front: Most common location for an ectopic pregnancy?

Back: Ampulla of the Fallopian tube.

Front: Definition of Asherman Syndrome?

Back: Intrauterine adhesions (synechiae) following over-aggressive D&C, leading to infertility.

Front: Cause of Vulvar Vestibulitis?

Back: Inflammation of small glands in the vestibule causing severe entry dyspareunia.

Front: Significance of a high mitotic count in a smooth muscle tumor of the uterus?

Back: Suggests Leiomyosarcoma rather than Leiomyoma.

Front: Common site of origin for Serous Tubal Intraepithelial Carcinoma (STIC)?

Back: Fimbria of the Fallopian tube.

Front: Histology of Comedocarcinoma (DCIS subtype)?

Back: Central necrosis with calcifications within the duct.

Front: Preferred staging system for Gynecologic cancers?

Back: FIGO Staging.

Front: Most significant prognostic factor in Breast Cancer?

Back: Axillary lymph node status.

Front: Effect of Triple Negative Breast Cancer on prognosis?

Back: Poor prognosis (ER-, PR-, HER2-).

Front: Hormone changes in Menopause?

Back: Decreased estrogen, significantly increased FSH (loss of negative feedback).

Front: Typical age for testicular torsion?

Back: Neonates or adolescents (bell-clapper deformity).

Front: Distinction between Seminoma and Non-seminomatous tumors regarding radiosensitivity?

Back: Seminomas are highly radiosensitive.

Front: Typical immunohistochemistry for Dysgerminoma?

Back: OCT3/4, PLAP, and c-KIT (CD117).

Front: Risk factors for Vulvar Squamous Cell Carcinoma (Non-HPV related)?

Back: Long-standing Lichen Sclerosus in elderly women.

Front: Definition of Chronic Prostatitis?

Back: Recurrent UTIs or pelvic pain with/without bacterial growth in prostatic secretions.

Front: Relationship between breast density and cancer risk?

Back: Increased radiographic density correlates with higher risk.

Front: Effect of breastfeeding on breast cancer risk?

Back: Protective (decreases lifetime estrogen exposure).

Front: Most common cause of Acute Mastitis?

Back: Staphylococcus aureus (usually during breastfeeding).

Front: Pathology of Periductal Mastitis?

Back: Subareolar inflammation seen mostly in smokers (Vitamin A deficiency leads to squamous metaplasia).

Front: Gross description of Mucinous (Colloid) Carcinoma of the breast?

Back: "Blue-gray" gelatinous mass.

Front: Precursor lesion to most Ovarian Surface Epithelial tumors?

Back: Inclusion cysts or fimbrial epithelium.

Front: Histologic feature of Clear Cell Adenocarcinoma of the ovary?

Back: Large cells with clear cytoplasm and "hobnail" cells.

Front: Definition of Endolymphatic Stromal Myosis?

Back: Low-grade endometrial stromal sarcoma that infiltrates vessels.

Front: Definition of Decidualization?

Back: Changes in endometrial stroma in response to progesterone to prepare for implantation.

Front: Distinction between Squamous Cell Carcinoma and Adenocarcinoma of the cervix?

Back: Both caused by HPV, but SCC arises from the exocervix and Adeno from the endocervical glands.

Front: Common cause of spontaneous abortion in the first trimester?

Back: Chromosomal abnormalities (e.g., aneuploidy).

Front: Definition of Placenta Accreta?

Back: Placental attachment directly to the myometrium due to a defective decidua.

Front: Difference between Placenta Increta and Percreta?

Back: Increta invades myometrium; Percreta invades through to the serosa/bladder.

Front: Leading cause of Maternal Death in the developed world?

Back: Thromboembolism or Preeclampsia complications.

Front: Histologic marker for Choriocarcinoma?

Back: Proliferation of cytotrophoblasts and syncytiotrophoblasts without villi.

Front: Mechanism of Hemolytic Disease of the Newborn?

Back: Maternal IgG antibodies cross the placenta and attack fetal RBCs (Rh incompatibility).

Front: Definition of Hydrops Fetalis?

Back: Severe fetal edema caused by anemia (immune or non-immune).

Front: Most common benign neoplasm of the fallopian tube?

Back: Adenomatoid tumor.

Front: Clinical sign of Hydrocele?

Back: Transillumination of the scrotum.

Front: Presentation of Testicular Lymphoma?

Back: Most common testicular mass in men >60; usually Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma.

Front: Target of Trastuzumab (Herceptin) in breast cancer?

Back: HER2/neu (ERBB2) receptor.

Front: Precursor to Invasive Lobular Carcinoma?

Back: Lobular Carcinoma In Situ (LCIS).

Front: Management of LCIS?

Back: Observation or Tamoxifen (considered a risk factor rather than a direct precursor).

Front: Hallmark of Paget's Disease of the Vulva?

Back: Intraepidermal spread of malignant cells; usually no underlying invasive cancer (unlike breast).

Front: Most common cause of vulvar "strawberry spots" on the cervix?

Back: Trichomonas vaginalis infection.

Front: Characteristic feature of Lymphogranuloma Venereum (LGV) in the chronic phase?

Back: Fibrosis and "esthiomene" (vulvar elephantiasis).

Front: Primary histologic feature of Granuloma Inguinale (Donovanosis)?

Back: Donovan bodies (intracellular safety-pin shaped bacteria) in macrophages.

Front: Type of epithelium replaced in Vaginal Adenosis?

Back: Squamous epithelium is replaced by columnar (glandular) epithelium.

Front: Most common site for Vulvar Melanoma?

Back: Labia minora and clitoris.

Front: Defining histologic feature of Hidradenoma Papilliferum?

Back: Complex papillary structures lined by a double layer of cells (myoepithelial and secretory).

Front: Primary risk factor for Malignant Mixed Müllerian Tumor (MMMT)?

Back: Prior pelvic radiation therapy.

Back: Adult-type Granulosa Cell Tumor.

Front: DICER1 mutations are commonly associated with which pediatric ovarian tumor?

Back: Sertoli-Leydig Cell Tumor.

Front: The "Two-Hit Hypothesis" in BRCA1/2 related breast cancer refers to?

Back: An inherited germline mutation followed by a somatic loss of the remaining wild-type allele.

Specific Histological Variants

Front: Classic histologic feature of "Hobnail Cells" is seen in?

Back: Clear Cell Carcinoma of the ovary or endometrium.

Front: Defining histological feature of an Adenomatoid Tumor?

Back: Vacuolated mesothelial cells forming gland-like spaces (often in the epididymis or fallopian tube).

Front: Presence of "Call-Exner-like" spaces in a male testicular tumor suggests?

Back: Sertoli Cell Tumor.

Front: Hallmark of Microcystic Adnexal Carcinoma of the vulva?

Back: Deeply invasive nests of small cysts and cords with perineural invasion.

Front: Definition of "Starry Sky" appearance in the reproductive system?

Back: Burkitt Lymphoma (can involve the ovaries or testes).

Advanced Male Reproductive Pathology

Front: Term for the precursor lesion of most Testicular Germ Cell Tumors?

Back: Germ Cell Neoplasia In Situ (GCNIS).

Front: Which testicular germ cell tumor does NOT arise from GCNIS?

Back: Pediatric Yolk Sac Tumor and Teratoma.

Front: Primary immunohistochemical marker for Seminoma?

Back: OCT3/4 and SALL4.

Front: Histologic appearance of Spermatocytic Tumor (formerly Spermatocytic Seminoma)?

Back: Three cell populations (small, medium, and giant) with "spireme" chromatin.

Front: Typical behavior of a Spermatocytic Tumor vs. a Classic Seminoma?

Back: Spermatocytic Tumors occur in older men and rarely metastasize.

Front: Clinical significance of "High-Grade Prostatic Intraepithelial Neoplasia" (HGPIN)?

Back: Precursor to prostatic adenocarcinoma; requires close follow-up but not immediate surgery.

Clinical Correlation & "Pearls"

Front: What is the "Schiller-Duval body" analogous to in embryology?

Back: A primitive glomerulus.

Front: Definition of "Pseudopityriasis Rosea" in the context of Syphilis?

Back: The generalized maculopapular rash of secondary syphilis involving the palms and soles.

Front: Cause of "Violin-string" adhesions in Fitz-Hugh-Curtis Syndrome?

Back: Chronic inflammation and scarring of the liver capsule following PID.

Front: Most common cause of a "Cold" (non-functional) nodule in the testis?

Back: Any solid testicular mass should be considered malignant until proven otherwise (orchiectomy).

Front: Significance of a "Blue Dot Sign" on the scrotum?

Back: Torsion of the appendix testis (benign, non-surgical).

Front: What is the "Whiff Test" used for?

Back: Diagnosis of Bacterial Vaginosis (addition of KOH produces a fishy odor).

Breast: High-Level Concepts

Front: Definition of "Flat Epithelial Atypia" (FEA) in the breast?

Back: Replacement of native terminal duct lobular unit cells by one or more layers of atypical cuboidal/columnar cells.

Front: Most common cause of "Granulomatous Lobular Mastitis"?

Back: Possible autoimmune reaction or reaction to Corynebacterium (seen in parous women).

Front: Defining feature of "Inflammatory Breast Cancer" on biopsy?

Back: Dermal lymphatic invasion by tumor emboli.

Front: Histologic distinction of Medullary Carcinoma of the breast?

Back: Pushing borders, syncytial growth pattern, and prominent lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate.

Front: Significance of the "Phyllodes" leaf-like architecture?

Back: Overgrowth of the stromal component relative to the epithelial component.

Placental & Developmental Nuances

Front: Primary risk associated with a "Bipartite Placenta" (Placenta Bilobata)?

Back: Increased risk of vasa previa and postpartum hemorrhage.

Front: Definition of "Amnion Nodosum"?

Back: Small, yellow-white nodules on the fetal surface of the amnion; associated with severe oligohydramnios.

Front: Most common cause of "Single Umbilical Artery" (SUA)?

Back: Usually idiopathic, but associated with an increased risk of fetal chromosomal and renal anomalies.

Front: Pathogenesis of "Gestational Alloimmune Thrombocytopenia"?

Back: Maternal antibodies (anti-HPA-1a) cross the placenta and destroy fetal platelets.

Front: Characteristic finding in "Cervical Intraepithelial Neoplasia" (CIN) Grade III?

Back: Full-thickness loss of maturation and high nuclear-to-cytoplasmic ratio.


Front: IHC marker to differentiate Sex Cord-Stromal tumors from Epithelial tumors?
Back: Inhibin (Positive in Sex Cord-Stromal).

Front: Characteristic IHC profile for Embryonal Carcinoma?
Back: CD30 positive, CK positive, and OCT3/4 positive.

Front: IHC stain used to confirm a diagnosis of Mesothelioma in the tunica vaginalis?
Back: Calretinin.

Front: Use of p16 IHC in cervical pathology?
Back: Strong, diffuse staining indicates high-risk HPV (E7) activity.

Front: IHC marker for Clear Cell Carcinoma of the ovary?
Back: HNF-1-beta.

Clinical Syndromes & Drug Effects
Front: Term for "Ovarian hyperstimulation" causing massive cystic enlargement?
Back: Theca Lutein Cysts (associated with high hCG or fertility drugs).

Front: Classic drug cause of Endometrial Polyps?
Back: Tamoxifen.

Front: Reproductive side effect of Spironolactone in males?
Back: Gynecomastia (due to its anti-androgenic effects).

Front: Condition of the breast associated with long-term Cimetidine use?
Back: Gynecomastia.

Front: Definition of "Stein-Leventhal Syndrome"?
Back: The original name for Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome (PCOS).

Rare & Complex Variants
Front: Definition of "Large Cell Calcifying Sertoli Cell Tumor"?
Back: A rare Sertoli cell tumor associated with Carney Complex.

Front: Defining feature of "Serous Surface Papillary Carcinoma"?
Back: Primary peritoneal cancer that is histologically identical to ovarian serous carcinoma but the ovaries are minimally involved.

Front: Histologic hallmark of "Microglandular Hyperplasia" of the cervix?
Back: Proliferation of small glands, often associated with pregnancy or OCP use; mimics adenocarcinoma.

Front: What is a "Decidual Cast"?
Back: The entire thickened endometrial lining shed in one piece, often seen in ectopic pregnancy or sudden progesterone drop.

Front: Definition of "Placental Site Trophoblastic Tumor" (PSTT)?
Back: Rare tumor of intermediate trophoblasts; secretes Human Placental Lactogen (hPL) rather than hCG.

Specific Developmental Anomalies
Front: Definition of "Gartner Duct Cyst"?
Back: Remnant of the Wolffian (Mesonephric) duct on the lateral wall of the vagina.

Front: Definition of "Müllerian Agenesis" (Mayer-Rokitansky-Küster-Hauser syndrome)?
Back: Congenital absence of the uterus and upper 2/3 of the vagina in 46,XX females.

Front: Result of a "Bicornuate Uterus"?
Back: Incomplete fusion of the Müllerian ducts, resulting in a heart-shaped uterus with two horns.

Front: Origin of a "Paraovarian Cyst"?
Back: Remnants of the Wolffian duct located in the broad ligament (e.g., Hydatid of Morgagni).

Vascular & Inflammatory Extras
Front: Most common cause of "Granulomatous Orchitis"?
Back: Idiopathic (suspected autoimmune) or Tuberculosis.

Front: Pathogenesis of "Fournier Gangrene"?
Back: Necrotizing fasciitis of the perineum/scrotum; a surgical emergency.

Front: "Yellow-brown" pigment in a "Luteoma of Pregnancy"?
Back: Intracellular lipid.

Front: Clinical behavior of a "Luteoma of Pregnancy"?
Back: Benign, regresses spontaneously postpartum, but can cause maternal virilization.

Front: Definition of "Squamous Metaplasia of Lactiferous Ducts" (SMULD)?
Back: Periductal mastitis; keratin plugs the duct leading to abscess and fistula formation in smokers.

Final High-Yield Odds & Ends
Front: Primary risk factor for Primary Vaginal Melanoma?
Back: Unlike skin melanoma, it is not UV-related; etiology is unknown.

Front: Key difference between "Low-grade" and "High-grade" SIL (Squamous Intraepithelial Lesion)?
Back: LSIL typically regresses (HPV 6, 11); HSIL has a high risk of progression (HPV 16, 18).

Front: Primary symptom of "Torsion of the Appendix Testis"?
Back: Sudden onset localized pain with a palpable "blue dot" on the upper scrotum.

Front: Definition of "Prehn Sign"?
Back: Relief of scrotal pain with elevation (Positive in epididymitis, Negative in torsion).

Front: Most common cause of "Azoospermia" (no sperm) in cystic fibrosis?
Back: Congenital Bilateral Absence of the Vas Deferens (CBAVD).

Front: How to distinguish a Seminoma from a Dysgerminoma?
Back: They are histologically identical; the name depends on the gender of the patient (Seminoma in males, Dysgerminoma in females).

Front: Distinguishing feature of Embryonal Carcinoma vs. Yolk Sac Tumor markers?
Back: Embryonal is usually AFP negative (or low), while Yolk Sac is always AFP positive.

Front: Histologic hallmark of a Granular Cell Tumor of the vulva?
Back: S100 positive cells with abundant eosinophilic granules and pseudoepitheliomatous hyperplasia (mimics SCC).

Front: Primary difference between Follicular Cysts and Theca Lutein Cysts?
Back: Follicular cysts are usually solitary; Theca Lutein cysts are multiple, bilateral, and driven by high hCG.

Front: Key histologic distinction between Paget’s Disease of the Breast and Melanoma?
Back: Paget cells contain mucin (PAS positive) and are S100 negative.

Specific Molecular Pathology & IHC (Level 2)
Front: Which breast cancer subtype is most associated with the "Basal-like" gene expression?
Back: Triple-negative breast cancer (ER-, PR-, HER2-).

Front: Primary molecular driver in Phyllodes Tumors?
Back: MED12 mutations.

Front: Molecular hallmark of Alveolar Rhabdomyosarcoma (Sarcoma Botryoides variant)?
Back: t(2;13) or t(1;13) translocation involving PAX3 or PAX7.

Front: IHC marker used to distinguish between a Complete and Partial Mole?
Back: p57 (expressed in Partial Moles; absent in Complete Moles due to lack of maternal DNA).

Front: Genetic mutation associated with Sertoli-Leydig tumors in Pleuropulmonary Blastoma?
Back: DICER1 mutation.

Placental & Umbilical Cord Micro-Pathology
Front: Definition of Placental Infarction?
Back: Localized necrosis of villi due to compromised maternal blood flow (common in Preeclampsia).

Front: Histologic sign of a CMV infection in the placenta?
Back: "Owl’s eye" intranuclear inclusions in enlarged villous cells.

Front: Definition of Chorangioma?
Back: A benign, hamartomatous capillary hemangioma of the placenta.

Front: Significance of "Hoffbauer Cells" in the placenta?
Back: They are fetal macrophages found in the villous stroma.

Front: Consequence of "True Knots" in the umbilical cord?
Back: Can lead to fetal hypoxia and intrauterine fetal death (IUFD).

Clinical Signs & Eponyms
Front: What is "Schwartzman Reaction" in the context of pregnancy?
Back: A form of DIC and renal cortical necrosis triggered by endotoxins (often associated with septic abortion).

Front: Definition of "Arias-Stella Reaction"?
Back: Hypersecretory endometrial changes (enlarged nuclei) seen in pregnancy or ectopic pregnancy; mimics malignancy.

Front: Classic presentation of "Peyronie Disease"?
Back: Fibrous plaque in the tunica albuginea causing painful curvature of the penis during erection.

Front: Definition of "Kraurosis Vulvae"?
Back: Severe atrophy and shrinkage of the vulva, often the end-stage of Lichen Sclerosus.

Front: Cause of "Pseudohermaphroditism" in males?
Back: Most commonly Androgen Insensitivity Syndrome (46,XY with female phenotype).

Male Reproductive: Detailed Anatomy & Rare Tumors
Front: Primary histologic feature of "Intratubular Germ Cell Neoplasia"?
Back: Atypical germ cells limited to the seminiferous tubules (precursor to most GCTs).

Front: Most common site for a "Sperm Granuloma"?
Back: The eyeidymis (often follows a vasectomy).

Front: Definition of "Tunica Vaginalis Hydrocele"?
Back: Fluid collection between the parietal and visceral layers of the tunica vaginalis.

Front: Mechanism of "Testicular Infarction" in Torsion?
Back: Venous obstruction occurs first, followed by arterial compromise and hemorrhagic necrosis.

Front: Histology of "Condyloma Acuminatum" vs. "Condyloma Latum"?
Back: Acuminatum shows koilocytic atypia (HPV); Latum shows plasma cell-rich inflammation (Syphilis).

Advanced Breast: Rare Findings
Front: Definition of "Cystosarcoma Phyllodes"?
Back: An older term for Phyllodes Tumor, emphasizing its "leaf-like" and potentially malignant nature.

Front: Distinguishing feature of "Secretory Carcinoma" of the breast?
Back: Rare, low-grade cancer that produces abundant intracellular and extracellular secretions; seen in children (Juvenile Carcinoma).

Front: Common location for "Ectopic Breast Tissue"?
Back: The axilla (most common site along the milk line).

Front: Definition of "Mondor’s Disease"?
Back: Superficial thrombophlebitis of the breast veins; presents as a painful, cord-like mass.

Front: Significance of "Atypical Lobular Hyperplasia" (ALH)?
Back: Similar to LCIS but cells fill <50% of the acini in a lobule; indicates increased cancer risk.

Final Synthesis: USMLE "Must-Knows"
Front: Most common cause of "Scrotal Pain" in a 20-year-old?
Back: Epididymitis (usually Chlamydia or Gonorrhea).

Front: Most common cause of "Scrotal Pain" in a 12-year-old?
Back: Testicular Torsion.

Front: Definition of "Hematocle"?
Back: Blood in the tunica vaginalis, usually following trauma.

Front: Result of "Chronic Endometritis" on fertility?
Back: Increases risk of ectopic pregnancy and infertility due to tubal scarring.

Front: Hallmark of "Postpartum Pituitary Necrosis" (Sheehan Syndrome)?
Back: Failure to lactate and loss of pubic hair due to ischemic necrosis of the anterior pituitary.
