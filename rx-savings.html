How to Save Money on Prescriptions

Prescription drug prices can vary significantly between pharmacies, even in the same neighborhood. Understanding how to navigate these price differences is the key to significant savings.

Top strategies to save money on your medications:

  • Compare prices: Always check prices across different pharmacy chains.
  • Use discount programs: Leverage free coupons and manufacturer programs.
  • Generic alternatives: Ask your doctor if a lower-cost generic version is available.
  • Avoid retail markup: Bypass expensive retail pharmacy markups with direct-to-consumer platforms.

MediHelp helps you find the lowest price instantly by comparing multiple platforms.

Why Drug Prices Vary Between Pharmacies

Most patients are surprised to learn that prescription drug prices can vary by over 100% between pharmacies on the same street. Factors such as pharmacy-benefit manager (PBM) contracts, retail markups, and inventory costs all play a role in the price you pay.

Why Drug Price Comparison Matters

Drug prices are not regulated in the same way as other medical services, meaning the price you pay can change based on the pharmacy's network, location, and specific discount contracts.
