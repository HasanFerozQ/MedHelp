Frequently Asked Questions

Is the MediHelp Now extension completely free?

Yes! The Chrome extension is 100% free to download and use. Searching for codes, checking NPIs, and running bill audits does not cost anything.

Where does your data come from?

We source our datasets directly from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). Information like NPI and NCCI edits are synced from public CMS databases to ensure accuracy.

Do you store patient PHI (Personal Health Information)?

Absolutely not. MediHelp Now only requests publicly available billing codes and provider numbers. All bill auditing happens completely locally on your machine, so NO patient information is ever sent to our servers. We are fundamentally built for HIPAA-compliance by design.

Can I use this on a Mac or Safari?

The extension currently operates strictly on Chromium-based browsers like Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Brave. You can use it on any operating system (Mac, Windows, Linux) as long as you use a supported browser.
