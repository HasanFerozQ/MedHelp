## CASE 01 — Calf Pain After Two Blocks
Scenario: A 62-year-old man with diabetes and heavy smoking history has reproducible calf pain after walking that resolves with rest; pulses are diminished.

Q1. Most likely diagnosis is:  
A. Lumbar spinal stenosis  
B. Peripheral arterial disease with intermittent claudication  
C. DVT  
D. Cellulitis  
Answer: B

Q2. Best initial bedside test is:  
A. Ankle-brachial index (ABI)  
B. D-dimer  
C. Venous duplex ultrasound  
D. Echocardiogram  
Answer: A

Q3. An ABI most consistent with PAD is:  
A. 1.2  
B. 0.95  
C. 0.70  
D. 1.4  
Answer: C

Q4. First-line management includes:  
A. Supervised exercise + smoking cessation + antiplatelet/statin therapy  
B. Urgent bypass surgery for all patients  
C. Long-term antibiotics  
D. No therapy needed  
Answer: A
